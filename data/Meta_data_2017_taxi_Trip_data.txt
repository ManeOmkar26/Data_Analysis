Column Name		Data Type			Description
ID			Integer		Unique trip identification number.
VendorID		Integer		Code indicating the TPEP provider that supplied the trip record. (1 = Creative Mobile Technologies, LLC, 2 = VeriFone Inc.)
tpep_pickup_datetime	Datetime	Date and time when the taxi meter was engaged (trip start time).
tpep_dropoff_datetime	Datetime	Date and time when the taxi meter was disengaged (trip end time).
Passenger_count		Integer		Number of passengers in the vehicle, entered by the driver.
Trip_distance		Float		Distance traveled during the trip in miles as recorded by the taximeter.
PULocationID		Integer		TLC Taxi Zone where the trip began (pickup location).
DOLocationID		Integer		TLC Taxi Zone where the trip ended (dropoff location).
RateCodeID		Integer		Final rate code applied to the trip. (1 = Standard Rate, 2 = JFK, 3 = Newark, 4 = Nassau/Westchester, 5 = Negotiated Fare, 6 = Group 						Ride)
Store_and_fwd_flag	Categorical(Y/N Indicates whether trip data was stored in vehicle memory before transmission due to lack of server connectivity.
Payment_type		Integer		Method used to pay for the trip. (1 = Credit Card, 2 = Cash, 3 = No Charge, 4 = Dispute, 5 = Unknown, 6 = Voided Trip)
Fare_amount		Float		Base fare calculated by the taximeter based on time and distance.
Extra			Float		Additional charges such as rush-hour and overnight surcharges.
MTA_tax			Float		Fixed $0.50 Metropolitan Transportation Authority tax applied to eligible trips.
Improvement_surcharge	Float		Fixed $0.30 surcharge collected to support transportation infrastructure improvements.
Tip_amount		Float		Tip amount paid by the passenger. Credit card tips are automatically recorded; cash tips are not included.
Tolls_amount		Float		Total toll charges incurred during the trip.
Total_amount		Float		Total fare charged to the passenger, including taxes, surcharges, tolls, and recorded tips (excluding cash tips).
